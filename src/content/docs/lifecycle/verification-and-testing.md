Verification confirms that completed work actually satisfies the documented requirements and acceptance criteria.

## What this stage is

After a phase is implemented, it is checked before it is marked complete. Verification compares the real result against the phase's acceptance criteria rather than assuming the work is correct.

## What to check

- Build or startup succeeds.
- Runtime behavior works as expected.
- Functional requirements are satisfied.
- Responsive behavior works on the required screen sizes.
- Accessibility basics are respected.
- RTL/LTR behavior works when relevant.
- Important regressions are checked.
- Known issues are documented.

## Rules to follow

- Do not mark work complete before verification.
- Compare results against documented acceptance criteria.
- Fix discovered issues before closing the phase.
- Record important failures or limitations.

## What should be completed before moving on

- Required checks pass.
- Acceptance criteria were reviewed.
- Known issues are documented.
- Project status reflects the real result.

Move to **Project Status Updates** after verification completes.
