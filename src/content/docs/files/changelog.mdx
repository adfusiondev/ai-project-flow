## Purpose

A dated timeline of what changed and why — the memory that survives across agents, sessions, and environments.

## When to use it

**Recommended** for Small projects; **required** for Standard and Advanced.

- **Create** — at the start of the project, alongside the other core files.
- **Update** — after a meaningful change, a completed phase, or an important decision.
- **Skip** — for Small projects with no changes worth recording.

## Required inputs

- What changed and why.
- The date and a short title.
- Verification results, decisions, or known limitations, when relevant.

## Reads from and feeds into

- Reads from `PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `PLAN.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, and git history.
- Feeds into `PROJECT_STATUS.md` and `HANDOFF.md`.
