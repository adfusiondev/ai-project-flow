## What it is

`docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` converts the approved MVP into clear, verifiable requirements.

## Why it exists

Requirements are the reference point for both implementation and verification. They keep the MVP from growing silently and make it possible to check whether finished work is correct.

## When it should be created

After the MVP is defined and approved.

## When it should be updated

- When the MVP changes.
- When a requirement is clarified.
- When constraints change.

## What it should contain

- Functional requirements.
- Non-functional requirements (simplicity, readability, responsiveness, performance, accessibility).
- Content requirements.
- Design requirements.
- Localization requirements (Arabic and English, RTL and LTR).
- Portability requirements.
- MVP constraints.

## Practical example

This repository's `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` documents requirements such as FR-01 (Project Lifecycle Documentation), FR-06 (Copy Functionality), LR-02 (Text Direction), and PR-01 (Repository as Source of Truth).
