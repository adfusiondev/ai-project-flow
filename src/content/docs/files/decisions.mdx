## Purpose

A log of what was decided, why, which alternatives were considered, and the current status of each decision.

## When to use it

**Required** for Advanced projects; **optional** for Standard; **not used** for Small.

- **Create** — as soon as the first important decision is made.
- **Update** — every time an important decision is made, superseded, or rejected.
- **Skip** — while there are no choices worth recording.

## Required inputs

- The decision and its status: Proposed, Accepted, Superseded, or Rejected.
- The reason it was made.
- Alternatives considered, where relevant.

## Reads from and feeds into

- Reads from `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`, `docs/MVP.md`, and `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`.
- Feeds into `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, and `HANDOFF.md`.
