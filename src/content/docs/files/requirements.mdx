## Purpose

The single reference for what the project must do and how well — so finished work can be checked against it, and the MVP cannot grow silently.

## When to use it

**Required** for Standard and Advanced projects; **not used** for Small.

- **Create** — after the MVP is defined and approved.
- **Update** — when the MVP changes, a requirement is clarified, or constraints change.
- **Skip** — for Small projects where the MVP already defines the scope.

## Required inputs

- The approved MVP scope and its out-of-scope list.
- Functional requirements: what the project must do.
- Non-functional requirements: performance, accessibility, portability, and more.
- Content and localization requirements when the project is bilingual.

## Reads from and feeds into

- Reads from `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` and `docs/MVP.md`.
- Feeds into `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, and `PLAN.md`.
