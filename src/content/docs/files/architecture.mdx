## Purpose

Architecture is the most expensive choice to reverse. Recording it — including the rejected alternatives — stops future agents from re-deciding it without the original context.

## When to use it

**Required** for Advanced projects; **optional** for Standard; **not used** for Small.

- **Create** — after the requirements are complete, before implementation planning.
- **Update** — when the technology, application type, or technical responsibilities change.
- **Skip** — when the technical approach is already obvious or fully constrained by the MVP.

## Required inputs

- The approved requirements and MVP constraints.
- The chosen application type and technology.
- The high-level structure and responsibilities.
- Rejected alternatives and why.

## Reads from and feeds into

- Reads from `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`, `docs/MVP.md`, `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`, and `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
- Feeds into `PLAN.md` and `HANDOFF.md`.
