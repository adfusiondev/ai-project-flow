A geometric reconstruction of the OpenCode wordmark's design DNA applied to AI PROJECT FLOW — constructed rectilinear letterforms on a strict grid, heavy 6 u monoline, open counters, and a two-tone ink / chip scheme with a mid-word fade (6|7 split through the middle word PROJECT, mirroring the reference's mid-word split). No typeface is used; every letter is drawn from primitives (rects and butt-capped strokes).
